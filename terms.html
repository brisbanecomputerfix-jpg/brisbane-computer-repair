4. "No Fix, No Fee" Diagnostic Guarantee

We stand behind our technical expertise. Our "No Fix, No Fee" guarantee ensures you are protected:

When No Fee Applies: If our lead technician inspects your device and is technically unable to identify the root cause of the fault or provide a viable repair solution, no service fee is charged.

Exclusions & Clarifications:

  • If the technician accurately diagnoses the fault and proposes a definitive repair (e.g. damaged motherboard, cracked screen requiring replacement, or dead drive), but you choose not to proceed with the proposed repair due to device age or financial preference, our standard diagnostic fee applies.
  • If a device is deemed Beyond Economical Repair (BER) where replacement costs exceed machine value, the diagnostic inspection fee applies for the professional assessment provided.
  • If repair cannot proceed due to customer refusal to provide essential system credentials, BitLocker recovery keys, or Apple ID passwords necessary to perform service, standard diagnostic fees apply.

While our technician exercises professional care and uses sector-level non-destructive protocols, you are solely responsible for maintaining independent backups of your critical data.

To the maximum extent permitted under Australian Consumer Law:

  • We are not liable for any pre-existing corrupted sectors, catastrophic read/write head collapses, ransomware-encrypted partitions, or data loss occurring due to hardware failure prior to or during diagnostic handling.

  • For dedicated data recovery services, recovery success depends on physical magnetic/flash drive integrity. No recovery fee is charged if zero recoverable files are retrieved from a dead drive.

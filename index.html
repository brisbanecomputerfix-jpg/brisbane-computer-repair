No, never. We do not charge travel or call-out fees to visit your home or office across metro Brisbane. You only pay our flat fixed service fee ($150–$170 depending on the job) and any replacement parts you approve upfront.
What does the $150–$170 fixed service fee include?
The fee covers our mobile travel, thorough on-site diagnosis, and the majority of software, virus cleanup, optimization, or labor repair tasks. Replacement parts (such as an SSD or battery) are quoted transparently at retail cost with zero markup.
How fast can a technician visit my location?
We respond promptly, typically within 5 minutes between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM, 7 days a week. Same-day mobile repair slots are available across Brisbane inner city and western suburbs.

Yes, absolutely! We take pride in our friendly, respectful patience. We avoid confusing jargon, walk you through solutions step-by-step, and write down simple notes you can keep by your desk.
What is an official Bank Clearance Report?

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

1. Introduction

Brisbane Computer Repair ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (brisbanecomputerrepair.au) or engage our local computer repair services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is necessary to provide you with our services. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, physical address (for mobile repairs), and email address.
  • Device Information: Passwords or PINs for your devices, but only when strictly required to perform diagnostics, repairs, or data recovery.
  • Service History: Records of the issues reported and services performed on your devices.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To contact you regarding your repair request or service updates.
  • To dispatch our technician (Ike) to your local Brisbane address.
  • To log into your device for the sole purpose of repairing or recovering data.

4. Data Security & Passwords

We understand that your computer holds sensitive personal data. We will never browse, copy, or retain your personal files (photos, documents, financial records) unless explicitly instructed by you as part of a Data Recovery service. Device passwords are kept secure and are destroyed or forgotten immediately after the repair is completed.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is strictly kept within our business for operational purposes, or disclosed only if required by Australian law.

4-Step Emergency Action Protocol for Swollen Batteries

When a lithium-ion battery begins outgassing and ballooning, immediate action is necessary to prevent thermal runaway and permanent chassis deformation.

1

Disconnect AC Charger Instantly

Unplug your charger immediately. Feeding continuous current into degraded, swelling pouch cells pumps electrical energy into decomposing electrolyte, dramatically increasing the hazard of an internal short-circuit.

2

Never Push Down or Puncture

Do NOT press down on a lifting trackpad or try to push the bulge back in. Absolutely never pierce the foil pouch with a pin or screwdriver. Puncturing lithium pouch cells causes instant explosive self-ignition.

3

Store in a Fire-Safe Location

Place the laptop on an uncarpeted, non-flammable surface—such as a tiled floor, metal workbench, or kitchen benchtop—away from direct sunlight, paper, bedding, and curtains while awaiting pickup.

4

Battery Chemistry & Physics

Why Do Lithium-Ion Laptop Batteries Swell?

Modern laptops use Lithium Nickel Manganese Cobalt Oxide (NMC) or Lithium Cobalt Oxide (LCO) polymer pouch cells. Unlike older cylindrical 18650 metal cans, pouch cells have a flexible aluminium foil laminate envelope designed to save space and weight.

Over hundreds of recharge cycles—or accelerated by continuous elevated heat in Brisbane's humid climate—the liquid electrolyte undergoes chemical breakdown through parasitic reactions. This outgassing produces a mixture of carbon dioxide (CO2), carbon monoxide (CO), and volatile hydrocarbons.

Designed Safety Failure: The swelling is actually an intentional safety mechanism. The pouch stretches to trap combustible gases inside rather than allowing an uncontained high-pressure explosion.

🔥 The Heat Soak Factor in Brisbane

Brisbane's summer ambient heat combined with blocked cooling fans and using laptops on soft beds traps heat at 65°C–80°C internally. Elevated thermal stress doubles the rate of chemical electrolyte degradation.

⚡ Continuous 100% Float Charging

Leaving laptops permanently docked at 100% state of charge keeps cell voltage high (~4.2V per cell). High voltage combined with warmth accelerates cathode oxidisation and gas production.

⚠️ Mechanical Chassis Stress

Because modern ultra-thin laptops have zero internal clearance, expanding cells exert massive pressure against the underside of the trackpad, bending keyboard deck plates and stripping aluminium casing threads.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions About Laptop Batteries

Everything you need to know about safety hazards, Apple top-case replacement policies, airline flight rules, and repair timelines.

Is a swollen laptop battery dangerous? Can it explode?
Yes. Swollen lithium-ion batteries are considered severe fire hazards. The swelling indicates that the chemical electrolyte has decomposed into volatile, combustible gases (carbon monoxide, hydrogen, and organic hydrocarbons). While the pouch is designed to stretch to contain these gases, any sharp impact, pressure, or internal micro-puncture can ignite thermal runaway, releasing toxic smoke and aggressive fires reaching 1,000°C. If your battery is swollen, stop using the laptop immediately and book professional extraction.
Why does Apple quote $600 to $900+ to replace a MacBook battery?
Apple and Apple Authorised Service Providers (AASPs) do not replace individual battery cells on modern MacBook Air and MacBook Pro models. Their corporate repair mandate requires replacing the entire aluminium top-case assembly—which bundles the aluminium deck, keyboard, and trackpad together—driving the cost up to $600–$900+. We use non-conductive chemical adhesive solvents to safely de-bond only the battery cells, installing brand-new OEM cycle-zero cells for $160–$280 while keeping your original keyboard and chassis intact.
Why is my trackpad stiff or impossible to click?
On MacBooks, Dell XPS, HP Spectre, and Razer Blade laptops, the battery is situated directly beneath the glass trackpad. When the pouch cells swell, they expand vertically, pressing firmly against the underside of the trackpad micro-switch or haptic actuator. This pressure eliminates the mechanical clearance required to click. In over 95% of cases, extracting the swollen pack immediately restores normal click action without requiring trackpad replacement.
Can I bring a laptop with a swollen battery on an airplane (Qantas / Virgin)?
No. Australian Civil Aviation Safety Authority (CASA) regulations and international airline safety rules (including Qantas, Virgin Australia, and Jetstar) strictly forbid carrying damaged, defective, or swollen lithium batteries anywhere on an aircraft—neither in cabin carry-on nor checked luggage. Security screening at Brisbane Airport can confiscate or deny boarding to bulging devices. You must replace the swollen battery before flying.
How long does a laptop battery replacement take at your Brisbane lab?
Standard screw-in batteries (found in Dell, Lenovo ThinkPad, HP, and ASUS models) generally take 45 to 90 minutes. Glued MacBook Pro and MacBook Air procedures take approximately 2 to 3 hours to allow proper chemical dissolving of the industrial adhesive, thorough tray cleaning, new cell installation, and post-assembly thermal calibration.
What causes laptop batteries to degrade and swell so fast in Brisbane?
Brisbane's humid subtropical climate is a major contributing factor. Elevated ambient summer temperatures combined with using laptops on soft surfaces (like beds or sofas) blocks bottom ventilation vents, keeping internal temperatures at 70°C–85°C. Heat accelerates electrolyte degradation by up to 200%. Keeping laptops continuously plugged in at 100% charge also stresses the cathode chemistry, inducing premature cell outgassing.
